Coodsz Menu Guard v1.0.0 (by Coodsz) - for players on the plain Coop client

WHAT IT FIXES
Your game freezes when you walk into a village or town: Escape and Enter do nothing,
and settlement names float around the map.
That happens when Coop sends one of the parties in that village or town to your game without all
its data.
This module stops the village/town screen from freezing on that party. The broken entry
just stays blank and the rest of the menu works.

ONLY THE PLAYERS need it. The server does not need it, and it does not stop you joining.
Coodsz Coop and Friends, TAOM and AD1259 launcher users already have it (added on Connect).
The first time you start the game with it, Bannerlord rebuilds its shaders once (it does that
whenever the mod list changes).

HOW TO INSTALL
1. Close the game.
2. Copy the DedicatedServer.CoodszMenuGuard folder from this zip into your game's Modules folder:
   ...\steamapps\common\Mount & Blade II Bannerlord\Modules\
   (so you have Modules\DedicatedServer.CoodszMenuGuard\SubModule.xml)
3. Right-click Modules\DedicatedServer.CoodszMenuGuard\bin\Win64_Shipping_Client\CoodszMenuGuard.dll,
   Properties, tick Unblock at the bottom (if you see it), OK.
4. Open the Bannerlord launcher, go to Mods, tick "Coodsz Menu Guard" and keep it BELOW Coop.
5. Play and join as usual.

If it ever catches the problem, it writes a line to:
Documents\Mount and Blade II Bannerlord\Client Diagnostics\CoodszMenuGuard.log
To remove it, untick it in the launcher or delete the folder.
