## New

**Coodsz Host can protect player kingdoms from AI wars.** Open **Advanced server rules** on the Settings page and pick when AI kingdoms may start a war with a kingdom your players are in.

- **Only while their players are offline** uses Coop's own protection, so nothing new runs on the server.
- **Always** keeps AI kingdoms from proposing or declaring war on any kingdom a player rules or serves as a vassal, whether that player is online or not. Mercenaries don't count, since they have no say in a kingdom's wars.
- **Off** lets the AI declare war on anyone.

The choice starts on what your Coop setting already was, so updating changes nothing until you pick something else. Wars your players start, a player attacking first and rebellions still happen with every choice. There is no forced peace either, so you never see a war pop up and end right after. Always works within a few seconds of Save settings. Switching between the other two needs a server restart.

The Always choice is adapted from ModderLords by Ryvius, with his permission.

New text is translated into all six languages. Click **Install update** in the tool, or unzip over your folder and choose Replace.

Code from CoopKeep by aderimo (MIT) is used for the server events, backups, bans, settings catalogue and compatibility list, and for the Home, Players, Cheats, Map, Encyclopedia and Guides pages. See THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md in the zip.
