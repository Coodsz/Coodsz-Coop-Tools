## A license for the tools

- **The tools now have a license.** They stay free to use for playing and hosting with your friends and community. Making money with them, decompiling them, reuploading them or sharing changed copies needs written permission from Coodsz.
- **It shows once.** The first time you open a tool you read it and click **I agree**. One I agree covers all Coodsz tools, and you are only asked again if the license changes. The full text is in LICENSE.txt next to the program.

## Fixes

- **UIExtenderEx missing a file.** When UIExtenderEx is missing one of its files (an unfinished Steam download or an antivirus), the game showed "Compiled Prefabs submodule could not be loaded due to a dependency conflict". **Check mods** now says which file is missing and how to get it back, and until then **Connect** turns off UIExtenderEx's Compiled Prefabs for that game, so it starts cleanly. The file is put back when the game closes.
- **Steam not ready on the host.** When Steam was not open or logged in as the server started, Coodsz Host Home kept saying the Steam lobby was coming. It now says the lobby did not start and to open Steam and press **Restart**.

New text is translated into all six languages. Click **Install update** in the tool, or unzip over your folder and choose Replace.
