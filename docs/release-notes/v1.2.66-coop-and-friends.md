## The TAOM War of the Ring bar works for players who join

**Players see the War of the Ring bar live.** On a TAOM server run with Coodsz Host, players now see the War of the Ring bar on the map as soon as the full war starts, and its numbers move with the server's. Before, the bar only showed after a rejoin and then its numbers stayed frozen from the moment they joined.

Their game also follows the server's war phase now, so TAOM's peace rules (like no peace between enemy sides during the full war) match what the server does.

It works by itself, with nothing to set up. The host needs this version of Coodsz Host, and players need the client add on that comes with this version of the Coodsz launchers. Without TAOM nothing changes.

## A note and screenshots in your reports

**Tell Coodsz what happened, and add screenshots.** Send my logs, Send crash report and Send server crash report now have two optional extras:

- **Tell us what happened**: a box for your own explanation, up to 2000 characters. Your Windows name, PC name, Steam ids, addresses and passwords are taken out of it the same way as from the logs.
- **Add screenshots**: up to 5 PNG or JPG pictures. You can also copy a picture and press **Ctrl+V** in the window. Each one shows as a small picture with a remove button. Very large pictures are made smaller, and hidden details like the camera, place or time a picture was taken are removed. The pictures themselves are sent as they are, so check them for anything private first.

Both are optional, so Send with nothing added works like before. **Save a copy instead** keeps your text and screenshots in the zip too.

## Fixes

**Save settings no longer stops on an empty number box.** An older server-config.json without a newer setting, like autoRestartHours, left that box empty in Coodsz Host, and Save settings then stopped with "must be a number" and saved nothing. Now an empty box leaves that setting as it is, and the rest of your settings save.

**The launcher window opens inside your screen.** On a small screen or with display scaling, like 1366 px wide at 125%, the window used to open wider than the screen, with the **Install update** button off to the right. It now fits the screen when it opens.

**Coodsz Host stays smooth during a burst of server log lines.** The log box now draws new lines a few times a second, and in a big burst only the newest lines it keeps. Every line is still read for players, join notes and saves.

**The Cheats page lost its old More cheats section.** Everything in it is on the page above: Set gold is in the Gold category, Give influence is in the Clan category, the player cards show who is online, and the **Console** page has the console.

**Reporting a mod is easier to find.** A short line under the title, *Played with mods? Tell others if they worked.*, points to **Report a mod**, and each mod in your list now has a small **Report** link. The report window shows what is sent from the start, so one click on **Send** sends it. With no extra mod to rate, **Report a mod** now says so instead of doing nothing. Reports and crash reports also carry your Coop version when the setup has Coop turned off.

New text is translated into all six languages. Click **Install update** in the tool, or unzip over your folder and choose Replace.
