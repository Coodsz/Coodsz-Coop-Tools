### What is new in v1.2.13

**Both tools, Chinese, Korean and Japanese.** The tools now come in Simplified Chinese, Korean and Japanese too. Pick yours with the 🌐 button.

**Both tools, a newer Bannerlord.** When your Bannerlord is newer than 1.4.8, the tools now say so in the **Made for** card and under **Connect** or **Start server**, with how to switch back to 1.4.8 in Steam. TAOM and Coop 0.1.6 are made for Bannerlord 1.4.8. It is only a warning and turns nothing off.

**Server Tool, the terms button.** The **I accept** button in the Coop server terms window now shows in your language.

**Client Launcher, Coop goes back into Modules.** When the Client Launcher sets a Coop folder aside, a Coop nightly too, it now puts it back into Modules when you close the launcher, or on its next start after a crash. Before, a Coop nightly could stay in the game folder as `Coop-kept-0.1.8`.

![The Coodsz TAOM download, one zip that unzips into a folder with the Server Tool and the Client Launcher](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Coodsz/Coodsz-TAOM/main/images/Coodsz-TAOM-new-download.png)

### How to update

Click **Install update** in the Server Tool and the Client Launcher. Or download `Coodsz-TAOM-Tools-v1.2.13.zip` and copy each program over the old one.

New text is translated into all nine languages.
