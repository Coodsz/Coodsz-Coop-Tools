## Crash reports ask for your Discord name

**Coodsz can reach out to you about your report.** Send crash report and Send my logs now have a box for your Discord name right above Send, in Coop and Friends, the client launcher and Coodsz Host. Send waits until the box has a name. Coodsz uses it to contact you about the report, and people who can read the report can see it.

**You only type it once.** The tool keeps the name on your PC and fills it in the next time. Save a copy still works without a name.

New text is translated into all six languages. Click **Install update** in the tool, or unzip over your folder and choose Replace.

Code from CoopKeep by aderimo (MIT) is used for the server events, backups, bans, settings catalogue and compatibility list, and for the Home, Players, Cheats, Map, Encyclopedia and Guides pages. See THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md in the zip.
