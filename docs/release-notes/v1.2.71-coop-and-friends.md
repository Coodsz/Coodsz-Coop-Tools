## Full mod list link, and big overhauls marked Broken

**See every mod in one place.** The mods page in Coop and Friends, the client launcher and Coodsz Host has a new **Full mod list** link. It opens the community list on GitHub, with every mod, its status and what players report.

**Big overhauls show as Broken.** The built in mod list now marks big overhauls as Broken, because only TAOM and AD1259 work in Coop, through their own Coodsz tools. Eagle Rising, Burning Empires, Realm of Thrones, The Old Realms and Shokuho need a major overhaul compat patch before they can work.

New text is translated into all six languages. Click **Install update** in the tool, or unzip over your folder and choose Replace.

Code from CoopKeep by aderimo (MIT) is used for the server events, backups, bans, settings catalogue and compatibility list, and for the Home, Players, Cheats, Map, Encyclopedia and Guides pages. See THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md in the zip.
