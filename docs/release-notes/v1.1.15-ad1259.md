### What is new in v1.1.15

**Both tools, Chinese, Korean and Japanese.** The tools now come in Simplified Chinese, Korean and Japanese too. Pick yours with the 🌐 button.

**Both tools, a newer Bannerlord.** When your Bannerlord is newer than 1.4.8, the tools now say so in the **Made for** card and under **Connect** or **Start server**, with how to switch back to 1.4.8 in Steam. AD1259 and Coop are made for Bannerlord 1.4.8. The Client Launcher still keeps **Connect** off on a newer game, but now says how to switch back instead of telling you to update the tool.

**Server Tool, the terms button.** The **I accept** button in the Coop server terms window now shows in your language.

**Server Tool, vote time.** **Show more Coop settings** on the **Settings** page now has the kingdom decision vote time.

**Server Tool, Copy setup tip.** The tip on the **Copy setup** button no longer says it copies the save and the map. It copies the mods, ports and MCM. Pick a save and a map for the new setup after.

### How to update

Click **Install update** in the Server Tool and the Client Launcher. Or download `Coodsz-AD1259-Tools-v1.1.15.zip` and copy each program over the old one.

New text is translated into all nine languages.
