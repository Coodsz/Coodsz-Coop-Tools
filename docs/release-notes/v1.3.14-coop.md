### What is new in v1.3.14

**Both tools, Chinese, Korean and Japanese.** The tools now come in Simplified Chinese, Korean and Japanese too. Pick yours with the 🌐 button.

**Both tools, a newer Bannerlord.** When your Bannerlord is newer than 1.4.8, the tools now say so under **Connect** or **Start server** and in the Server Tool's **Versions** card, with how to switch back to 1.4.8 in Steam. The tools and Coop 0.1.6 and 0.1.7 are made for Bannerlord 1.4.8. It is only a warning and turns nothing off.

**Server Tool, the terms button.** The **I accept** button in the Coop server terms window now shows in your language.

**Server Tool, newer Coop settings.** **Show more Coop settings** on the **Settings** page now has the kingdom decision vote time. On a Coop nightly server it also has how long AI parties leave a player alone after a battle, what happens to clans when their kingdom is destroyed, and **Preferred battle hosts**. There you add players who are online or in the picked save, or paste Steam IDs and click **Add Steam IDs**, then put them in order with **Move up** and **Move down**. The first listed player who has finished loading hosts the battle. Streamer mode hides their Steam IDs. Settings your server's Coop is too old for are greyed out with Needs Coop 0.1.7 or Nightly only, and the Server Tool never writes them to `mod-config.json`.

### How to update

Click **Install update** in the Server Tool and the Client Launcher. Or download `Coodsz-Coop-Tools-v1.3.14.zip` and copy each program over the old one.

New text is translated into all nine languages.
