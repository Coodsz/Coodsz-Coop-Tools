### What is new in v1.0.14

**Both tools, Chinese, Korean and Japanese.** The tools now come in Simplified Chinese, Korean and Japanese too. Pick yours with the 🌐 button.

**Both tools, a newer Bannerlord.** When your Bannerlord is newer than 1.4.8, the tools now say so in the **Made for** card and under **Connect** or **Start server**, with how to switch back to 1.4.8 in Steam. Burning Empires and Coop are made for Bannerlord 1.4.8. It is only a warning and turns nothing off.

**Server Tool, the terms button.** The **I accept** button in the Coop server terms window now shows in your language.

**Server Tool, pause and 2x for players.** **Start server** now turns on players' time buttons (pause and 2x) when an older `server-config.json` has no setting for them. Before, players on those servers could not pause or use 2x. A host who turned them off keeps them off.

**Client Launcher, Coop goes back into Modules.** When the Client Launcher sets a Coop folder aside, a Coop nightly too, it now puts it back into Modules when you close the launcher, or on its next start after a crash. Before, it moved extra Coop copies into the game folder for good.

### How to update

Click **Install update** in the Server Tool and the Client Launcher. Or download `Coodsz-Burning-Empires-Tools-v1.0.14.zip` and copy each program over the old one.

New text is translated into all nine languages.
